Glass Repair, Screens & Window Balances

Restore safety, clarity, and easy window operation without the cost of full window replacement. We replace foggy insulated glass, rebuild torn screens, and repair internal window balances.

Window Glass Restoration

Glass Repair & Foggy Window Replacement

When seal failure occurs in double-pane or triple-pane windows, moisture penetrates between the glass layers, causing unsightly condensation, haze, and mineral streaks. You don't have to tear out the entire window frame—we replace just the failed insulated glass unit (IGU)!

  • Save up to 70% compared to replacing full vinyl or wood window units
  • High performance Low-E coatings with argon gas fill for insulation
  • Precision thickness matching for single-hung, double-hung, and casement sashes

Window Balance Repair & Replacement

Does your window slam shut, feel heavy to open, or refuse to stay upright? The internal window balance mechanism has likely snapped or lost tension. We service all balance types to restore silky smooth, safe opening and closing.

  • Spiral balances (tilt and non-tilt styles)
  • Channel block-and-tackle balances
  • Constant force coil balances, pivot bars & sash locks
