Custom Glass Craftsmanship

Bespoke Shower Doors & Enclosures

Transform your everyday shower into a luxury sanctuary. Engineered with heavy architectural tempered glass, premium stainless steel & solid brass hardware, and laser-accurate measurements.

Seamless Luxury

Frameless Shower Doors

At C&C Glass & Window Repair, we take immense pride in the precision and craftsmanship of our frameless shower enclosures. Without bulky metal frames, your bathroom instantly gains light, depth, and a clean modern aesthetic.

We use heavy 3/8" or 1/2" architectural tempered glass with high-precision beveled or polished edges. Every panel is custom cut to align with out-of-plumb walls, tile sills, and knee walls for a completely watertight fit.

  • Single swinging doors, inline panels, 90-degree corners & neo-angles
  • Heavy solid brass self-centering hinges and structural clamps
  • Custom handle styles: mitered corners, ladder pulls, towel-bar combos
Versatile & Space-Saving

Sliding Shower Glass Doors

Experience the perfect combination of contemporary design, effortless glide, and spatial efficiency. Our modern sliding systems eliminate swing clearances, making them the ultimate upgrade for compact bathrooms or modern walk-in setups.

Equipped with industrial-grade stainless steel top rolling rods, precision ball bearings, and anti-jump safety stoppers, our sliding doors glide silently and maintain stability through years of daily use.

Smooth Whisper-Glide Rollers
Bypass or Single Slider Systems
At-Home Spa Luxury

Custom Steam Shower Enclosures

Create an authentic wellness retreat in your private bathroom. Custom steam showers require high engineering standards: full floor-to-ceiling glass seals, specialty high-temperature seals, and precision transom vents.

Our operable glass transoms allow you to regulate steam and humidity with ease, venting moisture when done while keeping 100% of therapeutic warmth trapped while relaxing.

  • Floor-to-ceiling airtight silicone sealing systems
  • Pivotable transom vents with magnetic latches
  • Compatible with all steam generators and aromatherapy outlets
